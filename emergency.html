Emergency Contacts

Important numbers for South Africans in Ireland — save this page

← Back to Home

In a life-threatening emergency, always call 112 or 999. These numbers work from any phone, even without credit.

Emergency Services

Emergency (Fire, Ambulance, Gardaí)

112 / 999

Free from any phone. Works without SIM or credit. Available 24/7.

Gardaí (Police) — Non-Emergency

1800 666 111

Garda Confidential Line. For non-urgent reports, concerns, or information.

South African Embassy, Dublin

+353 1 661 5553

Consular assistance, passport emergencies, and support for SA citizens. Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin 2.

Crisis & Mental Health Support

Pieta House — Suicide & Self-Harm

1800 247 247

Free, 24/7 crisis counselling. No referral needed. Walk-in centres available.

Samaritans — Emotional Support

116 123

Free, 24/7. Confidential listening and support. You don't have to be suicidal to call.

Crisis Text Line

Text HELP to 50808

Free 24/7 text-based support. Trained volunteers. Anonymous and confidential.

Domestic Violence

Women's Aid — National Freephone Helpline

1800 341 900

24/7 support for women experiencing domestic violence. Confidential, free, and non-judgemental.

Health Services

HSE Live — Health Information

1800 700 700

Health advice and information. Mon-Fri 8am-8pm, Sat-Sun 9am-5pm. Free call.

National Poisons Information Centre

01 809 2166

24/7 advice on poisoning emergencies. For healthcare professionals and the public.

Legal & Housing

FLAC — Free Legal Advice Centres

1890 350 250

Free legal information and advice. Covers employment, immigration, housing, and family law.

Threshold — Housing Advice

1800 454 454

Free housing advice for tenants. Help with notices to quit, rent disputes, and homelessness prevention.

South Africa — Specific

DIRCO — Dept of International Relations

+27 12 351 1000

SA Department of International Relations and Cooperation. For consular emergencies when the Dublin Embassy is closed.

SA Embassy Dublin (Repeat)

+353 1 661 5553

Passport renewals, emergency travel documents, notarial services, and assistance for SA nationals in distress.

Information believed correct as of July 2026. If a number has changed, please let us know.
