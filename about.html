Our Story

South Africans in Ireland was founded in 2020 to connect the growing SA community across the Emerald Isle. What started as a WhatsApp group has grown into a community of 5,000+ members across Dublin, Cork, Galway, Limerick, and beyond.

From helping newcomers find their feet to organising braais in the park and screening every Springbok match, we've built something special — a home away from home for every South African who's made Ireland their base.

Our Mission

To support South Africans settling in Ireland, celebrate our shared heritage, and build lasting friendships across the Rainbow Nation diaspora.

Our Values

Community First

Everything we do is for the community. No profit motive, no gatekeeping — just South Africans helping South Africans.

Ubuntu

I am because we are. We believe in shared humanity and lifting each other up, no matter where in SA you're from.

Inclusivity

All South Africans are welcome — every province, every language, every background. This is the Rainbow Nation abroad.

Braai & Belonging

Nothing brings people together like fire and food. We create spaces where you feel at home, thousands of kilometres from home.
