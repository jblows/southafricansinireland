Visa & Immigration

Your complete guide to living legally in Ireland as a South African

← Back to Resources
Disclaimer: Last updated July 2026. Fees, salary thresholds, and processing times change regularly. Always verify with official sources before making decisions.

Critical Skills Employment Permit

The Critical Skills Permit is the most common route for SA professionals moving to Ireland. It's designed for highly skilled occupations in shortage.

  • Minimum salary: €38,000 for roles on the Critical Skills list, €64,000 for all other eligible roles
  • Valid for 2 years, after which you can apply for Stamp 4 (open work permission)
  • Your spouse/partner gets Stamp 1G (open work permission) immediately
  • No labour market test required
SA Tip: Start your application before leaving SA. You'll need police clearance from SAPS which can take 6-8 weeks. Get it apostilled at the High Court in Pretoria.

General Work Permit

For roles not on the Critical Skills list with salaries above €34,000.

  • Requires a labour market needs test (employer must advertise the role)
  • Valid for 2 years, renewable for 3 years
  • After 5 years, eligible for Stamp 4

IRP Card (Immigration Registration)

Once you arrive in Ireland, you must register with immigration within 90 days.

  • Dublin: Book online at burghquayregistrationoffice.inis.gov.ie
  • Outside Dublin: Contact your local Garda station
  • Bring passport, work permit, proof of address, and €300 fee
  • You'll receive your IRP card (Irish Residence Permit) — this is your legal proof of residence
SA Tip: Dublin IRP appointments book out fast. Check the site at 10am on Thursdays when new slots open. Some community members share alerts in the WhatsApp group.

Stamp 4 (Open Work Permission)

Stamp 4 gives you permission to work without a permit — any employer, any job.

  • Available after 2 years on Critical Skills Permit
  • Available after 5 years on General Work Permit
  • Also available through Irish citizen spouse/partner
  • Renewable every 2 years until eligible for citizenship

Family Reunification

Bringing your family to Ireland from South Africa.

  • Spouse/partner can join you once you have your permit
  • Children under 18 included in the application
  • Financial threshold: evidence you can support your family without state assistance
  • Processing time: 6-12 months (plan ahead!)

Citizenship by Naturalisation

After living in Ireland for 5 years (with 1 year continuous residency before applying), you can apply for Irish citizenship.

  • Total reckonable residency: 5 years in the last 9 years
  • Must have 1 continuous year in Ireland immediately before applying
  • Fee: €175 application + €950 on approval
  • Processing time: 12-23 months currently
  • Ireland allows dual citizenship — you keep your SA passport
SA Tip: Irish citizenship also gives you EU citizenship — freedom to live and work in any EU country. Combined with your SA passport, you'll have excellent global mobility.
