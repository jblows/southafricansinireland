Healthcare

Understanding the Irish health system and getting the care you need

Public vs Private Healthcare

Ireland has a two-tier system. Unlike SA where medical aid is essential, Ireland's public system covers everyone who is "ordinarily resident" (living here with intent to stay 1+ year).

  • Public (HSE): Free hospital care, subsidised GP visits (or free with Medical Card), free maternity care
  • Private (Health Insurance): Faster access, choice of consultant, private hospital rooms. Costs €1,000-€3,000/year per adult
SA Tip: Don't assume you need private health insurance immediately. The public system, while slow for non-urgent issues, handles emergencies and maternity very well. Many SA families go without private insurance for the first year.

Registering with a GP

Your first step in Irish healthcare — find and register with a local GP (General Practitioner).

  • GPs are private practitioners — you pay per visit (~€50-€70)
  • Search on Google Maps or ask community members for recommendations
  • Some GPs have closed lists — call around until one accepts you
  • GP is your gateway to specialists (referral system, like SA)

Medical Card & GP Visit Card

  • Medical Card: Free GP visits, prescriptions, hospital care. Income-tested (~€184/week for single, €266/week for couple)
  • GP Visit Card: Free GP visits only. Higher income threshold
  • All children under 8 get a free GP Visit Card regardless of income
  • Apply online at mymedicalcard.ie

Hospital & Emergency Care

  • A&E (Emergency): free if referred by GP, €100 charge if self-referred (waived for Medical Card holders)
  • Public hospital care: free for everyone once admitted
  • Waiting lists for non-urgent procedures can be long (months to years)
  • For emergencies: dial 112 or 999

Prescriptions & Pharmacy

  • Drug Payment Scheme caps out-of-pocket pharmacy costs at €80/month per family
  • Register at any pharmacy — no assignment needed
  • Many SA medications are available under different brand names
  • Pharmacists can provide minor illness consultations (similar to SA)

Dental Care

  • Dental care is mostly private in Ireland
  • PRSI contributions entitle you to one free dental check + cleaning per year (after 5 years of contributions, or for some PRSI classes from year 1)
  • Costs: check-up ~€50-€70, filling ~€80-€150, extraction ~€100-€200
  • Consider dental insurance if you need significant work

Mental Health Support

  • GP: First port of call — can prescribe and refer to HSE mental health services (free)
  • MyMind.org: Affordable counselling (sliding scale from €30/session)
  • Pieta House: Free crisis support for suicidal thoughts or self-harm
  • Samaritans: 116 123 (free, 24/7, anonymous)
  • Text 50808: Free crisis text support
SA Tip: Moving countries is stressful — culture shock, missing family, dark winters. It's completely normal to struggle. Irish mental health services are stigma-free and accessible. Reach out early if you're finding it hard.
