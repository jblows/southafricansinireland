Getting Your PPS Number

Your PPS (Personal Public Service) number is like your SA ID number for tax and social welfare purposes. You need it before you can legally work.

  • Apply at your nearest Intreo Centre (Social Welfare office)
  • Bring: passport, IRP card, proof of address, proof of reason to need PPS (job offer letter works)
  • Usually issued within 1-2 weeks
  • You'll be given an emergency tax rate until Revenue processes your details
SA Tip: Don't wait for your PPS to start job hunting. Apply for it as soon as you have an address. You can accept a job offer and provide the PPS to your employer once received.

CV Formatting for Ireland

Irish CVs differ from SA ones. Key differences:

  • No photo required (unlike some SA companies)
  • 2 pages maximum — keep it concise
  • No ID number, marital status, or date of birth
  • Include a brief personal statement at the top
  • "References available on request" is fine — no need to list them
  • Tailor your CV for each application (Irish recruiters notice generic ones)

Where to Find Jobs

  • IrishJobs.ie — the main Irish job board
  • Indeed.ie — broad listings, good filters
  • LinkedIn — essential for professional roles, most Irish recruiters are active here
  • Jobs.ie — another popular board
  • PublicJobs.ie — government and public sector roles
  • GlassDoor — useful for salary benchmarks

Recruiters Who Understand SA Qualifications

Some recruiters are familiar with South African qualifications and work experience:

  • CPL Recruitment — large Irish agency, handles many international placements
  • Hays Ireland — global agency with good tech/finance roles
  • Morgan McKinley — strong in financial services
  • Sigmar — good for IT and engineering roles
SA Tip: Irish employers generally recognise SA degrees without issue, but if asked, you can get a QQI (Quality and Qualifications Ireland) comparability statement. Engineering and medical professionals may need specific body recognition.

Remote Work & Tax Implications

  • Ireland has a strong remote work culture post-COVID
  • If working for an Irish employer remotely, standard PAYE rules apply
  • Working remotely for a non-Irish company? You may still owe Irish tax if you're tax resident here
  • You become Irish tax resident if you spend 183+ days in Ireland in a tax year
  • e-Working tax relief: claim expenses for home office (broadband, heating, electricity)

Salary Expectations

Ireland generally pays well, especially in tech, pharma, and finance. Keep in mind:

  • Minimum wage: €13.50/hour (2025)
  • Average salary: ~€49,000
  • Tech roles: €55,000 - €120,000+
  • Tax is progressive: 20% up to €42,000, then 40% (plus USC and PRSI)
  • Use tools like ie.talent.com/tax-calculator to estimate take-home pay
