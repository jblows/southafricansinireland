The Irish School System

Irish education is structured differently from SA. Here's the breakdown:

  • Primary School: Junior Infants to 6th Class (ages 4-12) — equivalent to Grade R to Grade 6
  • Secondary School: 1st Year to 6th Year (ages 12-18) — equivalent to Grade 7 to Grade 12
  • Transition Year (TY): Optional year between Junior Cycle and Senior Cycle — no SA equivalent, it's a gap year within school

SA Grade to Irish Year Conversion

  • SA Grade 1 = Ireland 1st Class (Primary)
  • SA Grade 7 = Ireland 1st Year (Secondary)
  • SA Grade 10 = Ireland Transition Year or 5th Year
  • SA Matric = Ireland Leaving Cert (6th Year)

Enrolling Your Child

  • Contact schools directly — each school manages its own admissions
  • Bring: child's passport, previous school reports, proof of address
  • Schools cannot refuse based on nationality or religion (Education Admissions Act 2018)
  • If all local schools are full, contact the local Education and Training Board (ETB)
SA Tip: Apply to multiple schools. Popular schools in good areas often have waiting lists. Start the process months before you move if possible. Some families arrive mid-year and find places — don't panic.

Types of Schools

  • Catholic schools: Most common (still ~90% of primary schools). Non-Catholic children are accepted.
  • Educate Together: Multi-denominational, inclusive. Very popular with international families.
  • Gaelscoileanna: Teach through Irish language. Not recommended unless arriving young.
  • Private schools: Fees range €5,000-€25,000/year. Not necessary — Irish public schools are generally excellent.

SA Qualification Recognition

For adults seeking qualification recognition:

  • QQI (Quality and Qualifications Ireland) — provides comparability statements for foreign qualifications
  • SA matric is generally well recognised for employment purposes
  • SA university degrees are recognised but get a QQI statement if applying for further study
  • Professional qualifications (engineering, nursing, accounting) may need specific body recognition

Third-Level Education

  • Universities, Institutes of Technology, and Technological Universities
  • EU fees apply after 3 years of legal residency (much cheaper than international fees)
  • SUSI grants available for qualifying residents
  • Springboard+ offers free/subsidised upskilling courses for adults
SA Tip: If you're on Stamp 4, your children qualify for "free fees" at third level after 3 years residency. This saves €15,000-€30,000+ compared to international student fees.
